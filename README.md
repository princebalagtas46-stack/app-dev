# My Favorite Series: Money Heist 💰

![Money Heist](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/6/6f/Money_Heist_poster.jpg)

## 📖 Overview
**Money Heist** (original title: *La Casa de Papel*) is a Spanish heist crime drama created by **Álex Pina**.  
The story follows a criminal mastermind known as **The Professor**, who recruits a group of eight skilled robbers to carry out ambitious heists on Spain’s Royal Mint and Bank.

## 💡 Why I Like It
- The story is full of suspense and unexpected twists.  
- The characters have deep backstories that make them memorable.  
- The theme of resistance and unity is inspiring.  

## 🧠 Main Characters
1. The Professor (Sergio Marquina)  
2. Tokyo (Silene Oliveira)  
3. Berlin (Andrés de Fonollosa)  
4. Nairobi (Ágata Jiménez)  
5. Rio (Aníbal Cortés)  

> *“The second any blood is shed, we’ll no longer be Robin Hood, but just a bunch of plain criminals.”* – **The Professor**
